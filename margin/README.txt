Margin - highlight, annotate and learn from what you read (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

INSTALL
1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent (the browser loads it from there;
   do not move or rename it afterwards).
2. Open chrome://extensions (edge://extensions, brave://extensions) and turn
   on Developer mode.
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose this "margin" folder.
4. In Margin's Details, turn on "Allow access to file URLs" so PDFs stored on
   your computer open in the reader.
The dashboard opens with a short getting-started guide.

WHAT IT DOES
- PDFs open in the Margin reader on their own address. Select text to
  highlight in one of four named colors, add a note, or translate just that
  word and save it to your vocabulary.
- Scanned pages are read automatically (on your computer) so their text can
  be selected too. A pen and highlighter mark anything by hand.
- Cite: finds the published record and gives the citation in APA, MLA,
  Chicago, Harvard or BibTeX; "Copy quote" adds the page reference.
- Export notes as Word, Markdown, or as a copy of the PDF with your
  highlights inside it, readable in any PDF program.
- Flashcards for saved words: word to meaning, meaning to word, or typing.
- Highlighting and notes also work on ordinary web pages.

BACKUP AND SYNC
Margin saves a copy of everything to Downloads/Margin backups once a day
(one file per weekday). To restore: Dashboard > Settings > Import a backup,
and pick the newest file there.
To keep two computers in step, choose a folder inside OneDrive, Google Drive
or Dropbox in Settings > Sync on each computer. Brave needs
brave://flags/#file-system-access-api set to Enabled first.

UPDATING
Replace the files in this same folder; Margin notices the new version within
a minute and reloads itself.

PRIVACY
Everything is stored in this browser profile. Text leaves your computer only
when you press Translate (Google Translate, fallback MyMemory, and
dictionaryapi.dev) and when a citation is looked up (Crossref).

Built on Mozilla PDF.js, Tesseract.js and pdf-lib (licenses included).
